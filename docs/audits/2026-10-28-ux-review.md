From surface 9 on, each surface also gets a UI polish pass (user's ask,
2026-10-28: high perceived quality, PLG, first-user experience), inside the DS.

Heuristic pass (Krug, Nielsen, severity 0–4) + copy pass against
[`copy-principles.md`](../copy/copy-principles.md) and `/stop-slop`, one surface
at a time, flags included. Every em dash in a surface's UI copy and in Archie's
lines goes (decided 2026-10-28); mock content (post bodies, idea titles) stays. Clear fixes land as one commit per surface on the branch
`claude/ux-copy-review-74de39`; what needs an arbitration is listed under
« To decide ».

## Findings

### 1. Shell — rail, topbar, Admin menu, new chat · 8/10 → 9/10

Fixed:

- **Sev 2 · Match with the real world.** An empty chat said _« Ask a follow-up,
  or refine a draft… »_ — there is nothing to follow up on. Every empty chat now
  says _« Paste a link, drop a file, or tell me what to post… »_ (it was behind
  `newChatHero` only).
- **Sev 2 · Recognition over recall.** A chat row's coloured dot is its
  Playbook's colour, said nowhere, and a long name was cut with no way to read
  it. The row's title now gives _« {name} · Playbook: {Playbook} »_.
- **Sev 2 · Consistency.** Every Acme Topic was labelled _Competitors_, including
  the ten built on Elena Verna's, Anthony Pierri's, Dave Gerhardt's and Lenny's
  posts — the Playbook's own influencers. They are now _Influencers_, and the
  Acme feed listens to both lanes.
- **Sev 1.** The Topic Feed's loading line said _« Reading what your competitors
  published… »_ while the page says competitors and influencers.

To decide:

- **Sev 2.** The influencer invitation in the Topic Feed is headed _« New source
  for this feed »_ even when the feed already reads influencers and the Playbook
  lists four. The code says it is shown on purpose either way; the heading is
  what contradicts the state. Proposal: hide it when the Playbook has
  influencers, or retitle it.
- **Sev 1.** The sidebar foot shows two icons side by side that both lead to
  _Send feedback_ (the bubble, and the first item of the cog menu).

### 2. Chat thread + composer · 6/10 → 8/10

Fixed:

- **Sev 3 · User control / consistency.** The Batch Studio's button says
  _« Extract ideas »_, then the new chat asked _« What should I do with it? »_
  about the same sources. Sources replayed from the Batch Studio now go straight
  to Find post ideas (`askSourceUse(…, { answer })`); a source added later in
  the chat is still asked about.
- **Sev 2 · Honesty.** The Batch Studio promised _« then draft every post »_;
  nothing drafts until you pick ideas. Its hint and third step now say so.
- **Sev 2 · Match with the real world.** The greeting offered to _« compare
  ideas, pick the strongest one »_ in a chat with no ideas, and a chat opened
  from the Batch Studio with Acme attached said _« Pick a Playbook any time »_
  (the greeting was seeded by whichever read came first). One greeting now:
  _« Hi. Drop a source or tell me what to post, and I'll draft from it in your
  Brand's voice. »_
- **Sev 2 · Recognition.** A long pasted text was named _« Pasted text »_, twice
  on the staging row (_Pasted text / Pasted text_) and again in the chat. It now
  takes its opening words (`pastedTextName`, shared), and the row gives its word
  count.
- **Sev 2 · Copy.** Extracted ideas were titled _« The unspoken constraint
  hiding in Our Q3 offsite notes: we cut onboarding from 9… »_ (the source name
  pasted into the title), with a body in the third person (_« A pattern Archie
  noticed… »_). The four templates no longer name the source; the card's source
  tag already does.
- **Sev 1.** The Batch Studio's sub-line repeated its first step word for word;
  removed. The drop overlay said _« upload »_ (banned verb). The seeded thread
  wrote _« playbook »_ in lowercase.
- **Copy.** 45 em dashes out of the chat's own lines (composer, pickers, Batch,
  Clip and Top-posts studios, errors, toasts), rewritten rather than swapped.

To decide:

- **Sev 2.** One extraction raises three signals at once: a toast _« 1 idea
  ready »_, the green composer bar _« 1 idea ready · View ideas »_, and the card
  in the thread. `contentPanel` already drops the toast and bar; worth doing
  without the flag.
- **Sev 2.** _Batch from a source_ promises a batch of drafts and stops at the
  ideas. A _Draft all_ after a Batch extraction would keep the starter's promise.

### 3. Right panel · 8/10 → 9/10

Fixed:

- **Sev 2 · Error prevention.** The Ideas filters offered kinds with no idea
  (_Hooks 0_, _Stats 0_), each a dead end to an empty list. A kind with no idea
  is no longer offered (it was the `contentPanel` behaviour only); the active
  filter stays so it can be undone.
- **Sev 2 · Consistency.** The Clips empty state said to pick _« Cut into
  clips »_; the option is _« Cut it into clips »_.
- **Sev 1.** The Clips select-all band was labelled _« Clips · 5 clips »_ under
  a _Clips_ tab; it says _« Select all · 5 clips »_.
- **Sev 1 · Copy.** Drafts empty state: _« Ask me for a batch »_ named no action
  you can take there; it now points at _Draft post_ on an idea. Sources empty
  state left out links and text. The Live connector badge, the draft link
  labels: em dashes out.

Noted for surface 5 (draft card): the character counter is an icon and a bare
number (_2721_, _-25_), the metric named only in the hover title.

To decide:

- **Sev 1.** In Sources, an idea's kind is forced to lowercase (_story_) by CSS
  to mirror the mention picker, while the Ideas tab shows _Story_.

### 4. Add-source modal · 7/10 → 9/10

Fixed:

- **Sev 2 · Error recovery.** Enter on a value that isn't a link did nothing:
  the button stayed grey and the error only showed on blur. Enter now shows the
  error, which reads _« Enter a web address, like acme.com/blog. »_.
- **Sev 2 · Error prevention.** _acme.com/blog_ was refused for lacking
  `https://`. A bare domain now gets `https://` and goes through.
- **Copy.** Toasts _« Link added. I'm reading it now. »_ and _« Text added. I'm
  reading it now. »_; the paste placeholder and hint, the recognised-service
  line and the empty connectors list lose their em dashes.

Not a regression: the console logs a few local 404s on load, on the base commit
as well (`scripts/dev-server.py` doesn't log paths). Worth a look on its own.

### 5. Draft card + inline edit + feedback · 7/10 → 9/10

Fixed:

- **Removed (user's call, 2026-10-28).** The character count chip under each
  draft (an icon and a bare number, hidden while editing) is gone, at rest and
  in edit mode. Going over a limit is still said by the validation above the
  card and the _Needs fixes_ tab. A first pass had made it live while editing;
  the user asked for it removed instead.
- **Copy.** Feedback toasts (_« Thanks. I'll use this next time. »_), the
  default-voice line, _Save as draft_ tooltip, the _Fixed. Ready to publish._
  infobox, the carousel limit warning, the image-style questions: em dashes out.

Left alone: the icon-only action rail mirrors prod's `post-card-actions`
(FEATURES §4), each icon with a tooltip. Rewritten-draft openers in
`draft-rewrite.js` are post content, not UI.

### 6. Schedule modal · 8/10 → 9/10

Fixed:

- **Sev 2 · Match with the real world.** One draft read _« From Thu, Oct 29, at
  LinkedIn's best time »_ above a slot on Tue, Nov 3: the date is the window's
  start, the slot lands on the network's next best day. It now reads _« Not
  before Thu, Oct 29… »_, which stays true whatever the slot. A saved rhythm
  reads _« Acme's rhythm: not before… »_ (colon, no em dash).
- **Copy.** The slot's aria-label loses its em dash.

Checked and left: the bulk _Schedule_ is disabled with a hover tooltip saying how
many drafts to fix first (FEATURES §4). The legacy modal (`newScheduleModal`
OFF) is kept as is, by decision.

### 10. Playbook creation in chat · 6/10 → 9/10

Fixed:

- **Sev 3 · First-user experience.** The page asks for a website, Archie says
  _« Drop your website URL below »_, and the URL field was the fourth and last
  row, under a preselected _« Start from files instead »_ (instead of what?).
  The field now leads: shortcut 1, focused on open, a link icon, placeholder
  _« Your website, like your-brand.com »_; the alternatives follow
  (`customFirst` / `customIcon` on inline-question, the keyboard follows the DOM
  order so 1–4 still map). _Start from files_ drops its _instead_.
- **Polish.** The three pills under the title (_Voice profile · Audience ·
  Visual identity_) repeated the sentence above them word for word, looked like
  buttons (white pill, border, shadow, bold) and set their text in grey-150.
  Removed. Archie's first line no longer repeats the subtitle either: _« No
  website? Pick another way in below. »_
- **Copy.** Step 2 said _« Pick the profile »_ (banned for account): _« Pick
  the account that will publish. »_ Eight strings of the flow lose their em
  dashes.

### 11. Image Studio: style creator + generator · 7/10 → 9/10

Fixed:

- **Sev 2 · Error recovery.** Save on an empty style listed both problems in a
  red banner at the bottom of the page, far from the fields, and asked for _« at
  least one »_ image under a hint saying _« 2 to 6 »_. Each field now says what
  it misses under itself (`.ap-form-message error`, the name input `invalid`),
  the page scrolls to the first one and focuses it, and each message clears as
  soon as the field is filled. The hint says _« Up to 6 images… »_.
- **Polish.** Three stacked cards for one short form became one card, its
  groups split by grey-20 hairlines. The lead reads _« A look I learn from your
  images, then draw Acme · Q2 marketing's new images in. »_ instead of _« It
  comes first whenever an image is made »_.
- **Sev 1.** In the generator, the _My style_ tag sat over a 70px tile and was
  cut to _« My styl »_ under the check badge; the caption under the grid
  already says _My style_, so the tile drops it (the gallery keeps its tag).
- **Copy.** _« Generate and I'll draw from the draft. You'll get 4 variations to
  pick from. »_; the style caption, the text-on-image tooltip and hint, the
  replace-image line and the dropzone label lose their em dashes.
