Overlay index

Every modal and panel that has no URL of its own, each behind a shareable link. The link opens the app and fires the overlay — ?openModal=<id> and ?openPanel=<id>, read by src/figma-capture.js.

Flags matter: an overlay behind a feature flag needs it ON in Admin first, or the link lands on the app with nothing open.

Modals

Open from anywhere — these need no target.

Right panel

The sliding panel's modes. They mount on a chat, so the link carries one.

Needs a target

These act ON something — a post, a Topic, a Playbook, a source — so there is nothing to show without one. Open them from the app.
